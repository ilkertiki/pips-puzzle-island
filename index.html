🏝️ Pip's Puzzle Island

Fun learning puzzles for kids aged 6 to 9. A new episode every day!

Join Pip and friends on a colorful island full of puzzles. Every episode has 10 puzzles across four subjects:

  • 🔢 Math: counting, adding & taking away, the number line
  • 🔷 Shapes & colors: shapes, patterns, odd one out
  • 🔤 Words & letters: spelling, missing letters, rhyming words
  • 🦁 Animals & nature: animal homes, what animals eat, riddles

Can you solve each puzzle before Pip? Pause, shout out your answer and see if you were right!
