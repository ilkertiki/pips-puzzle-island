Privacy Policy

Pip's Puzzle Island · Last updated: October 20, 2026

Who we are

Pip's Puzzle Island is a YouTube channel with learning puzzle videos for children aged 6 to 9. The videos are produced and uploaded by our own software, "Pip's Puzzle Island Uploader", which runs only on our own computer.

The uploader app and Google user data

The uploader uses the YouTube Data API to manage our own YouTube channel only. It is not offered to the public and no one else signs in to it. With the access we grant it, it:

  • uploads our videos and Shorts, and sets their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and "made for kids" setting;
  • adds our videos to our channel's playlist and updates our channel's description, banner and watermark;
  • reads our channel's own video list and settings to check that uploads worked.

The sign-in token is stored only on our own computer. We do not sell, share or transfer any Google user data, and we do not use it for advertising. The uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Viewers and children

We do not collect any personal information from viewers, including children. Our videos are watched on YouTube; we have no forms, accounts, comments, tracking or data collection of our own. Our channel and videos are marked "made for kids" on YouTube, so YouTube turns off comments and personalized ads on them. YouTube's own practices are covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

This website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics. GitHub may keep standard server logs as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Revoking access

The channel owner can remove the uploader's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
