About

Pip's Puzzle Island publishes free learning puzzle videos for children aged 6 to 9 on YouTube. "Pip's Puzzle Island Uploader" is our own internal tool for publishing those videos to our own channel. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

Using our videos

Our videos are watched on YouTube and are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. They are made for entertainment and practice and are not a replacement for school or a teacher. We do our best to make every puzzle correct, but we cannot promise that the videos are free of mistakes.
